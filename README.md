# max-pasho.github.io

**<https://max-pasho.github.io>**

The personal site of Max Pasho, a cancer biology student at the University of Alabama at Birmingham. It's the home base for the things I build: each project gets its own page, and every page links back here, so wherever someone lands, they can find the rest.

## What's here

- **Home:** who I am, featured projects and a short note on my research.
- **[Projects](https://max-pasho.github.io/projects/):** everything I've built or am building.
- **[About](https://max-pasho.github.io/about/):** education, research and interests.

## Projects

### Radon & Cancer Map *(in development)*

An interactive map of the United States that shows radon levels next to cancer rate statistics, made easy for everyday people to read and understand, not just scientists.

### [GRID](https://max-pasho.github.io/projects/grid/)

A terminal-first workspace for macOS: a real terminal, a file browser, an app launcher, a web browser and an offline map in one keyboard-driven window, styled after *Tron: Legacy* and *Tron: Ares*.

[Project page](https://max-pasho.github.io/projects/grid/) · [Download](https://github.com/max-pasho/grid/releases/latest) · [Source](https://github.com/max-pasho/grid)
