Research

Neuroblastoma

Undergraduate research · 2025–present

I study cell fate in neuroblastoma using zebrafish: how developmental stage, location in the body and the surrounding microenvironment push tumor cells toward neuronal, progenitor-like or apoptotic states. The work involves transgenic lines, screening crosses and imaging fluorescent reporters.

Colorectal cancer

Summer research

I helped evaluate an experimental drug for colorectal cancer, running Western blots and immunohistochemistry on tumor samples.
